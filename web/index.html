Victron solar controllers (Bluetooth)

In VictronConnect open each SmartSolar → cog → Product info → enable Instant readout via Bluetooth → Instant readout details: Show, and copy the encryption key here. The address is optional; use Find nearby to see what the hub can hear.

Caravan level

Bubble level with wheel and jockey heights. Connects to the van over Bluetooth, so it works at sites without internet.

Weather location

Tank calibration

Fill or empty a tank, then press the matching button to store the sender reading.

Temperature sensors

Bluetooth thermometers (ThermoPro TP357, Govee H5075, Shelly BLU H&T, Xiaomi with pvvx firmware) or wired DS18B20 probes. Warm a sensor in your hand to tell which is which.

Battery

Sync the shunt state of charge when you know the battery is full.

BMPRO OdysseyLink (Bluetooth)

Enter the OdysseyLink's Bluetooth address and press Connect; the hub remembers it. With bmproExplorer on, every frame is logged here – switch pumps in the Odyssey app and watch what changes.
